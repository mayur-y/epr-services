About Me

I am a Plastic Engineer and EPR Consultant with over 4 years of experience in Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR), Plastic Waste Management (PWM), Recycling, Sustainability Compliance, and Circular Economy initiatives.

I support Producers, Importers, Brand Owners (PIBOs), Plastic Waste Processors (PWPs), Small and Micro Producers (SIMPs), MSMEs, startups, and recycling organizations in achieving CPCB compliance through registrations, annual return filing, EPR credit management, compliance audits, and strategic advisory services.

Combining technical expertise in plastics, recycling, and sustainability with practical industry experience, I help organizations reduce compliance risks, improve operational efficiency, and build sustainable growth strategies.

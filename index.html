About Me

I am a Plastic Engineer and EPR Consultant with 4+ years of experience in Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR), Plastic Waste Management (PWM), Recycling, and Sustainability Compliance. I help Producers, Importers, Brand Owners (PIBOs), Recyclers or Plastic Waste Processors (PWPs), Small and Micro Producers, Sellers, Manufacturers, and Importers of Raw Materials (SIMPs), MSMEs and Startups achieve CPCB Compliance through EPR Registrations, Annual Return Filing, EPR Credit Management, and Regulatory Advisory. With a strong technical background in plastics and recycling, I provide practical solutions that simplify compliance, reduce business risk, and support sustainable growth.

FINILOOP | WASTE, Netherlands

Technical Contributor | City-level Plastic Waste Management Programme

Developed end-to-end technical process plans for plastic waste handling infrastructure, including machinery selection, plant layout design, and operational workflow optimisation. Contributed to scalable recycling models aligned with environmental standards and circular economy principles for urban waste management systems.

Composite Railway Sleepers from Plastic Waste | CIPET

Designed and developed plastic waste-based composite railway sleepers as a sustainable alternative to conventional timber and concrete sleepers. Fabricated specimens, conducted mechanical testing as per Indian Railways Standards, and performed comprehensive data analysis and technical documentation.

The project demonstrated improved flexural strength, impact resistance, and significant plastic waste diversion potential.

Indian Institute of Technology (BHU), Varanasi

School of Materials Science & Technology

Worked on the development and characterization of polyurethane-based nanocomposites for biomedical applications, including FTIR, SEM, and mechanical testing.

Also completed a project involving chemical modification of guar gum from hydrophilic to hydrophobic properties through functionalisation techniques and material characterization.

Plastics in Furniture Manufacturing | CIPET

Evaluated plastic materials for furniture applications based on mechanical properties, processing requirements, and end-use performance. Conducted ASTM and ISO testing of materials and prepared financial feasibility analysis for commercial-scale production.

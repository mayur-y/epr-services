About Me

I am a Plastic Engineer and EPR Consultant with over 4 years of experience in Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR), Plastic Waste Management (PWM), Recycling, Sustainability Compliance and Circular Economy initiatives.
I support Producers, Importers, Brand Owners (PIBOs), Plastic Waste Processors (PWPs), Small & Micro Producers (SIMPs), MSMEs and Startups in achieving CPCB compliance through EPR registrations, annual return filing, EPR credit management, compliance audits and regulatory advisory services.
By combining technical expertise in plastics and recycling with practical compliance experience, I help organizations reduce risks, improve operational efficiency and strengthen their sustainability performance.
